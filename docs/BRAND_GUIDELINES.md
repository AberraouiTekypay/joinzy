## 1. Brand Concept & Philosophy

**JOINZY = JOIN + social / youthful "-zy"**

Joinzy represents the pulse of physical, real-world community life across the Mediterranean and European startup and cultural ecosystems. It is **not** a generic corporate B2B SaaS, and **not** an overdesigned Gen-Z novelty. 

### Brand Attributes
- **Editorial & Warm**: Inspired by modern architectural, cultural, and community publications.
- **Mediterranean & Grounded**: Warm sand, sun-baked terracotta, olive sage, and deep charcoal.
- **High-Energy Social**: Fast, mobile-first, vibrant, and action-oriented.
- **Trust-Centric**: Focused on verified behavioral history rather than vanity metrics.

## 3. Typography Rules

### Font Families
- **Display & Headings**: `Space Grotesk` (Geometric, confident, modern editorial feel)
- **Body & Interface**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Crisp legibility, clean numbers, neutral geometry)

### Scale & Hierarchy
- **Hero Title**: 64px–96px / Leading: 1.02 / Weight: 900 (Black)
- **Section Headlines**: 36px–52px / Leading: 1.1 / Weight: 900 (Black)
- **Card Titles**: 18px–24px / Leading: 1.25 / Weight: 800 (Extrabold)
- **Body Large**: 18px–20px / Leading: 1.6 / Weight: 400 (Regular)
- **Body Regular**: 14px–16px / Leading: 1.5 / Weight: 400–500 (Regular / Medium)
- **Labels & Tags**: 10px–12px / Tracking: Wide / Weight: 700–800 (Bold / Extrabold uppercase)

## 4. Logo Usage Guidelines

The Joinzy wordmark (`Joinzylogo.png`) features:
- A custom lowercase `joinzy` typographic construction.
- Terracotta (`#E4572E`) for the dot and base hook of the letter `j`.
- Deep Charcoal (`#1F1F1D`) for the remaining letters.

### Logo Rules
1. **Never alter the color scheme**: Do not recolor the logo in blue, green, purple, or rainbow gradients.
2. **Clearspace**: Maintain at least half the height of the `j` around all sides of the logo.
3. **Backgrounds**: The logo looks best on Warm Sand (`#EDE6D9`), White (`#FFFFFF`), or placed inside a Warm Sand capsule when used on Charcoal (`#1F1F1D`).
4. **Attribution rule**: Always accompany the footer with the exact string:
   ```
   An EM300.co Company
   ```

## 5. Tone of Voice & Copywriting

### What We Say
- *"Find your people. Make things happen."*
- *"Don't just scroll. Go somewhere."*
- *"Your community doesn't need permission."*
- *"Reputation is earned."*
- *"Communities create the activity. Joinzy helps find the resources to make it happen."*

### What We Avoid
- **No startup clichés**: Never write "revolutionizing", "disrupting", "synergy", "paradigm shift", "unlocking the future", or "web3".
- **No SaaS jargon**: Do not describe Joinzy as an "enterprise event management platform" or "CRM pipeline".
- **No fake hype**: Never fabricate fake testimonials, fake stats, or fake star ratings.
