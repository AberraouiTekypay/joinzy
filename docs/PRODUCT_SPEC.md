# JOINZY Product Specification & Architecture

> **Product**: Real-World Community Network & Microsponsorship Marketplace  
> **Target Audience**: Grassroots communities, local businesses, enterprise sponsors  
> **Parent Organization**: EM300.co  

---

## 1. Problem Statement & Opportunity

### The Fragmentation Problem
Real-world grassroots communities are thriving across Morocco and France:
- AI builders in Casablanca
- Sunrise runners in Rabat
- BJJ athletes in Marrakech
- Designers in Paris & Lyon
- Foodies, tech founders, and creative collectivities

However, they are organized across fragmented channels:
1. **WhatsApp / Telegram**: High noise, zero persistent track record, impossible for sponsors to find or fund.
2. **Facebook Groups**: Declining youth engagement, algorithmically demoted offline activity.
3. **Legacy Event Tools (Meetup, Eventbrite)**: High ticketing fees, subscription walls for organizers, zero native sponsorship marketplace.

### The Joinzy Solution
Joinzy provides the dedicated infrastructure where organizers create groups for free, host gatherings, publish needed resources (venues, coffee, cash), and connect directly with businesses eager to back them.

## 2. Core Stakeholder Experience

### A. Community Organizers (100% Free)
- **Profile Hub**: Branded community page with member roster, past events, and metrics.
- **1-Click Needs Posting**: Post event requirements (e.g. "Space for 80 people", "100 coffees", "MAD 2,500 grant").
- **Automated Dossiers**: Instant, institutional-grade media kits generated directly from the community's verifiable platform track record.
- **Check-in Engine**: QR-based verified attendance check-ins that protect the organizer's integrity score.

### B. Participants (Free Discovery & Attendance)
- **Local Discovery**: Filter by city and vibe to find genuine gatherings.
- **Frictionless RSVP**: 1-click spot reservation with calendar integration.
- **Attendance History**: Verified participation record within favorite communities.

### C. Sponsors & Local Businesses (Pay-as-You-Go Marketplace)
- **No Subscription Required**: Businesses can sponsor a single dinner, run, or critique night without lock-in.
- **Direct Deliverables**: Guaranteed booth space, welcome keynote, logo placement, or product sampling.
- **Flexible Pledging**: Cash grants or in-kind donations (space, refreshments, equipment, prizes).
- **Post-Event Proof**: Detailed attendee counts, photo proofs, and cohort satisfaction benchmarks.

## 3. Long-Term Defensibility: The 7 Platform Graphs

Joinzy's competitive moat grows through the accumulation of 7 interlocking graphs:

```
Community Graph  (Who groups together)
      +
Event Graph      (What happens, where and when)
      +
Participant Graph (Who attends in real life)
      +
Sponsor Graph    (Who funds community culture)
      +
Venue Graph      (Physical spaces that host activity)
      +
Transaction Graph (Micro-grants and resource exchanges)
      +
Reputation Graph (Verifiable behavioral track record)
```

This creates an irreversible network effect where established communities cannot easily leave without abandoning their verified sponsorship credit rating.

## 4. Trust & Reputation Infrastructure

Rather than simplistic 5-star ratings prone to manipulation, Joinzy's reputation score is strictly based on **measurable platform behavior**:

1. **Events Completed Rate**: Percentage of published events that actually took place.
2. **Attendance Verification**: Ratio of verified physical QR check-ins vs RSVP count.
3. **Ghosting Penalty**: Severe reputation deduction for unannounced cancellations.
4. **Repeat Sponsor Score**: Percentage of businesses that renewed sponsorship with the community.
5. **Venue Stewardship**: Confirmation from venue partners that spaces were left clean and on time.

### Tiers
- **New** (0–2 completed events)
- **Established** (3–10 completed events, >70% verified attendance)
- **Verified Level 3** (10+ completed events, multiple sponsor renewals, spotless history)

## 5. Privacy & Data Ethics Policy

Joinzy is built on **permissioned, aggregated, and privacy-conscious** principles:
- **No Personal Data Selling**: Joinzy never sells attendee lists, phone numbers, or private emails.
- **Cohort-Level Analytics**: Sponsors only see aggregated demographic distributions (e.g. "44% Senior Engineers, 28% Founders").
- **Zero Surveillance**: No third-party tracking pixels or background device profiling.
